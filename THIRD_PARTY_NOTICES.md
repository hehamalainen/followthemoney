## Fonts

The interface optionally requests DM Sans and Manrope from Google Fonts, with system-font fallbacks. These font families are offered under the SIL Open Font License: [DM Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/dmsans/OFL.txt) and [Manrope](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/OFL.txt). No Google font binaries are bundled here. If fonts are downloaded and redistributed, preserve their applicable license and copyright files.

The film renderer can use fonts installed on the rendering machine. Apple system fonts are not bundled or relicensed by this project. Rendered films do not distribute the underlying font files.
