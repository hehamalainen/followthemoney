## Reproduce and maintain

Run the circulation builder before the directory builder:

```sh
python3 scripts/build_circulation.py
python3 scripts/build_coverage.py
```

`scripts/research/circulation-coverage.json` contains the relationship expansion. The directory combines `coverage-core.json`, `coverage-downstream.json` and `coverage-supplement.json` with the saved financial and circulation datasets. Source dates retain their documented precision; reporting-period dates must not be presented as publication or settlement dates. Rebuilding these committed inputs does not refresh prices or fill the financial gaps.

The expanded map preserves the original 59 relationships and 11 cases, adding 25 relationships and six cases. Commitments, funded equity, debt facilities, recognized revenue, guarantees and non-cash incentives are not interchangeable. Linked relationships do not prove that identical dollars circulate or that revenue is artificial; there is no consolidated circular-dollar total.

The committed widescreen and vertical films remain the original dated **43-counterparty / 59-relationship** edition. No media was changed for this audit. Their on-screen counts should not be read as the size of the expanded interactive map.

Financials describe the whole company, not AI-only earnings. Missing values are not zero. Scenarios and rankings are authored judgments, not forecasts, probabilities or projected stock returns. “Hidden champion” means a company passes the documented screen; it does not establish fair value or investor awareness. This project is a research tool, not investment advice.
